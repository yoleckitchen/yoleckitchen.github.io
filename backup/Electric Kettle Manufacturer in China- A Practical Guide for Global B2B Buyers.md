## 2. Verify Whether the Supplier Is a Real Manufacturer

One common challenge when sourcing online is identifying whether a company is:

- A factory
- A trading company
- A sourcing agent

A trading company is not necessarily a bad choice.

Sometimes they provide valuable market knowledge and supplier connections.

But if you are looking for OEM manufacturing, it is important to understand who controls production.

Before cooperation, ask:

- Where is your factory located?
- What products do you manufacture?
- How many production lines do you have?
- Which processes are handled internally?

A professional [electric kettle manufacturer in China](https://www.yolec-electronics.com/) should be able to explain its production process clearly.

## 5. Check Factory Production Capability

A product sample is important.

But mass production capability is more important.

Before placing a large order, buyers should understand:

### Production Capacity

Ask:

- What is your monthly output?
- How many production lines do you operate?
- How do you handle peak seasons?

### Manufacturing Process

Ask about:

- Injection molding
- Metal processing
- Assembly lines
- Product testing

### Quality Control

Ask:

- How are materials inspected?
- How are products tested?
- How are defects handled?

A factory that can produce one perfect sample may not always be able to produce thousands of units with the same quality.

## 6. Pay Attention to Product Quality and Certification Requirements

For electrical appliances, quality problems can directly affect a buyer’s brand reputation.

Before cooperation, buyers should discuss:

- Safety requirements
- Testing standards
- Required certifications
- Warranty expectations

Different markets may have different requirements.

For example:

- European buyers may focus on CE-related requirements.
- North American buyers may need different safety approvals.
- Other regions may have their own import regulations.

A good supplier should understand these differences before production begins.
